Sidebars are fine until the tree gets tall. Then you're expanding folders, squinting at names, and forgetting what you clicked to find.

`⌘K` opens the command palette. Type a scrap of a path, a collection name, or an action. Jump straight there.

## What it's good for

- Hopping to a request you saved last week
- Switching context without scrolling
- Triggering common actions when your hands are already on the keys

You don't have to live in the palette. Use it when the sidebar feels slow. Close it and keep editing.

Same idea as your editor's quick-open — pointed at API work.
