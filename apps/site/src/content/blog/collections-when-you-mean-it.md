History is the automatic layer. It catches what you sent so you can find it later.

Collections are different. They're the tree you build on purpose — the login flow you reuse, the staging suite you share with yourself next month, the folder named after a ticket.

## How it feels day to day

Send freely. History fills in. When a request is worth keeping, hit `⌘S` and put it where it belongs. Nest folders if you want. Don't if you don't.

## Local, like everything else

Collections live on your disk. No workspace invite. No "syncing…" spinner. Export when you need a backup or a handoff.

Automatic history for momentum. Collections for the stuff you intend to keep.
